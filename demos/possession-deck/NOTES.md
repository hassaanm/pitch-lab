# Possession Deck — notes
- **Rules as built:** 5 zones (Defense → Midfield → Attack → Box → Keeper) are enemies with HP and a fixed, telegraphed intent cycle: Press X (hits Composure, Control absorbs), Compact X (block; Through Ball / Needle Pass ignore it), Foul (draw 1 fewer). 3 energy, draw 5, block-style Control resets each turn. Keeper: ½ from normal cards, ×3 from Shots. Break the Keeper = goal → pick 1 of 3 cards → touchline stop (Physio / Coach perk / Training thins the deck) → new attack with all zones +20% HP. Composure 0 = turnover → counter mini-turn (2 energy, 4 cards, each card's 🛡 value raises a visible stop %), then a roll. 5' per turn, full time at 90'.
- **Visible odds:** card text shows real numbers vs the current zone (Rhythm, Off-balance, keeper rules coloured). Selecting a card ghosts the HP loss / "BREAKS!" on the zone plate and the Composure you'd lose. The only random roll (counter) shows its %. Undo (Z) restores the exact state, including draw order.
- **Core decision each turn:** spend energy on Progress now vs. Control to survive the Press — and, at the Box, whether to save Shots for the Keeper (×3) instead of using them on outfield zones.
- **Where the fun is:** the Keeper rule makes Shots feel like a real finishing decision; Tiki Coach + Tiki-Taka pass chains feel like building play. Breaking a zone mid-turn and carrying leftover energy into the next zone feels like a counter-attack surge.
- **Where it isn't (honest):** outfield zones still read as "HP sponges with a football name". Press/Compact are just StS attack/block. A zone breaking does not change how the next zone plays, so it's closer to "monsters in a row" than "an attack". Ideas: zones that react to how you broke the last one (wing vs centre), or overflow progress carrying forward.
- **Balance:** rough. Starter deck usually scores 1–2 per match; turnovers are rare unless you ignore Press.

## Polish pass
- **Structure:** rules now emit ordered events (each hit names its zone and HP left; the rival turn returns what it did). The view plays them as tweens on pitch actors; the HUD shows a pre-action copy of the state (`V`) and patches it only on each contact frame. Input is blocked while an action plays; tapping a card again queues it.
- **Possession is physical:** the card's player gets the ball by a pass (new teammates fade in at the support spot). Pass cards play the ball into space for the teammate; carries run with it; shots meet a blocker or the keeper's hands at the same point and time. The ball's height up a zone shows how much of it is broken.
- **Rival turn:** Press = a defender runs at the carrier, Composure drops on contact; Compact = they squeeze together, block appears when they arrive; Foul = slide tackle + whistle on contact.
- **Turnover:** the presser takes the ball off our carrier's feet and becomes the counter runner (keeper throws it to Dima). Defend cards send our two players back; Face it ends in a slide tackle, a Wak save, or a goal, each resolved when the ball arrives.
- **Cuts:** goal, counter reset and undo fade the players out, re-place them, fade back in. Celebration is a run, not a jump. End turn flies the hand to the discard pile; drawn cards appear when the card resolves.
- **Mechanic:** leftover Progress carries into the next zone (halved vs the Keeper). It makes a zone break feel like an attack surging on, and the preview shows "BREAKS! +N → next".
- **UI:** Rhythm moved to a badge on the pitch (no status-row reflow); keeper plate sits below the goal; defenders stand clear of the plate; hand fan no longer clips; key hints hidden under 400 px (the action row overflowed 375 px by 7 px).
- **Debug:** `?jank=1` exposes `window.__slow` (0.25 = quarter speed).
- **Review fixes:** slides and tackles lead with the boots (the sprite lies toward −facing with boots at the feet anchor, so the tackler faces the ball and puts its feet beside it); keeper dives lead with the gloves. The challenge comes from the ball's open side. A turnover deep in our half breaks out wide, so runner, tackler and Wak (on his line) stay apart; result banners sit over the top of the pitch. After a goal the new attack is swapped in while the scene is faded out. The preview runs the same rules on a copy ("BREAKS ×2! +7 → ATT").
- **Pace + input:** a normal card is ~0.7–0.8 s from tap to next tap (was 1.2–2.4 s); a rival turn ~0.75 s. The queue holds any number of cards (numbered badges); a single tap only selects. Cards still being dealt ignore taps. Build-up starts outside our box (v 0.81); on a deep Press or Foul, Wak shuffles across his line away from the challenge. The preview's "+N → zone" is what lands in the last zone reached, read off the copy's hit events. Result banners hide the zone/counter plate while they show and sit away from the contact.
- **Mechanics (review):** starting Composure Kickoff 40 → 24, Second half 26 → 14, so ignoring Control can cost a turnover; at the Keeper you always start the turn holding a Shot if the deck has one (it swaps for the last card drawn).
