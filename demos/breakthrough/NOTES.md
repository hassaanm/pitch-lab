# Breakthrough Puzzles — notes

- **Rules as built:** 5×7 grid, no dice. Each attacker moves once per turn (≤2 king steps through empty squares). The team gets **one pass per turn**, straight or diagonal, nobody on the line; a receiver who hasn't moved can still run after it. Shoot from the top 3 rows when a straight/diagonal lane crosses the goal line between the posts, outside the keeper's reach (his column ±1), with no bodies in it.
- **Defenders:** at the start of your turn each one telegraphs a path toward where the ball is *now*; they walk it when you end the turn, and an attacker on the path stops them. Finish a turn with the carrier next to any defender and you lose. Types: defender (1 step), sprinter (2), man-marker (2 toward his attacker, ignores the ball), sweeper (2, sideways only), slow giant (moves on odd turns only), keeper (slides 1 toward the ball's column).
- **Core decision each turn:** where to leave the ball at the end of the turn. That one square decides where every defender and the keeper will be next turn.
- **Verification:** `node demos/breakthrough/solve.mjs` runs a 0-1 BFS over the same `rules.js` the game uses. All 17 puzzles are solvable, their `par` matches, and none is solvable with one fewer turn. `-v` prints a solution. (The `package.json` here only marks the folder as ES modules for node.)
- **Where the fun is:** the "keeper covers ±1" rule makes every puzzle a show-left-score-right switch, which reads instantly and feels like real football (move the keeper, find the far post). Body-blocking a defender's arrow with a teammate is the best "aha".
- **Where it isn't:** because the keeper rule dominates, several puzzles feel like the same puzzle with different furniture. Par often rewards just standing still (end turn is free), so 3 stars can feel arbitrary. The man-marker barely matters in 2-turn puzzles because his move is locked in at turn start. Next try: shot lanes blocked by *defender arrows* (not just bodies), and puzzles where the keeper starts off-centre so baiting outfield players is the main idea.

## Polish pass

- **Animate, then commit.** Every action computes its result with `apply()`, plays the motion, and only then updates the rules state, HUD and log. Before, the state and HUD changed first and the sprites caught up.
- **The ball is physical.** It is always at a holder's boot (`vis.holder`) or in flight (`vis.ball`, in pixels). Passes leave the passer's boot and land on the receiver's boot, with no jump between the held and flight positions.
- **Tackle:** the tackler braces, slides until his foot meets the ball, and the TACKLED banner, flash and sound fire at contact. The ball squirts loose and the tackler gets up and collects it. Before, the ball stayed with the carrier.
- **Body block:** a stopped defender lunges into the attacker on his path. The attacker braces, and BLOCKED shows at the moment they meet. Before, a log line appeared after everyone had stopped. The rules now name the blocker (`info.blockers`).
- **Shot:** the keeper dives toward the lane and is at full stretch as the ball passes him, just short. Before, he dived the wrong way. GOAL fires when the ball reaches the net.
- **Undo and reset** rewind: players jog back and the ball travels back to its holder. A new puzzle, or a reset after a goal, fades the sprites out and in. Before, everything snapped into place.
- **Planning overlays** (arrows, red and gold squares) fade out while defenders walk or a shot flies, and fade back in for the new state.
- **Layout:** the pitch is sized once and resized only when the width changes, not when the phone's address bar moves. The hint and info rows have fixed heights, so "Show hint" no longer pushes the pitch down. The title row does not wrap, and the info row is no longer under the sticky bar.
- **Input:** buttons no longer flash grey on every animation; taps during motion are ignored. The "Clear shot!" toast that covered the Shoot button is gone, and a sound plays instead.
- **Clarity (fun pass):** core decision = where to leave the ball at the end of the turn. A red dashed ring now marks the square the defenders chase this turn (`state.aim`), so the bait is visible: pass away and the arrows still point at the ring. The How-to explains it.
- Dev: `?jank=1` adds `window.__slow` for slow motion and draws from the fallback timer, so the detector still works in a hidden tab. The telegraph ghosts no longer feed the detector (`noTrack`). Before, they hid real defender teleports.
- **Review fixes:** the shot now flies into `lane.cross.x`, the column the rule checks and the OPEN marker lights. Before, it aimed at `lane.goal.x` and could go through the keeper. The keeper stays down through GOAL, then gets up through `ready`. Tapping the preselected carrier keeps him selected, and the How-to now says he starts selected. The Keep chip sits above the carrier's head, so the chase ring is visible. Grey Shoot and Pass stay tappable and say why in the info row (no toasts). The pitch reserves the measured info and bar heights, re-measures once after the web font loads, and uses the visual viewport height. The Shoot and End labels sit over their odds pills, so the bar no longer overflows 375 px; that overflow had zoomed the whole page out. Chips, Undo and Reset are now at least 42 px. The keys line in the How-to shows only with a mouse.
