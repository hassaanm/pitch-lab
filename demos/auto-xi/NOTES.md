# Auto XI — notes
**Rules as built:** 5-match cup run, 10 hearts. Shop of 5 (1–3 gold by tier; odds improve with level). Reroll 1g, level up 4–6g for 3→6 outfield slots, 5 bench slots, sell for full refund. Three copies of a player combine into ★★ (×1.45 stats).
Traits count unique players: styles (Possession, Gegenpress, Catenaccio, Intensity, Flair) tier at 2/4/6, roles (Engine, Playmaker, Poacher, Wall, Speedster) at 2/4.
Placement matters: each role has a home line (60% out of position); Playmaker feeds the forward ahead, side-by-side Walls form a Partnership, Engines carry adjacent midfielders, Speedsters on a flank add Attack. No Engine pair = 2nd-half fade.
Board → team stats (Control/Attack/Finish/Defence/Press/Keeper) → Touchline-style possession odds → 8 scripted possessions per half, animated with an adapted copy of Touchline's MatchView. W/D/L + xG projection updates live as you place. Half-time = +4 gold and a fresh shop. Loss costs 2 + margin hearts, draw 1. Final draws go to penalties.
**Core decision each shop:** chase a trait tier vs. buy raw quality vs. save gold (interest) — and where on the board each player stands.
**Where the fun is:** the live projection reacting to every move (swap two players, watch win% jump) and hitting a ★★ or a 4-tier. Scouting a Catenaccio-4 opponent and building to beat it feels like coaching.
**Where it isn't:** the match itself is pure watching — once you hit Kick off you have zero input for ~40 seconds, and outcomes are low-scoring and swingy, so a good build can still lose 0–1 with no story. Pool is small (27 units), so rolls repeat; no opponent shop/adaptation at half-time; balance is hand-tuned, not tested over many runs.

## Polish pass
- **Shared match view.** Deleted the old `view.js` copy (teleporting `give()`, labels before motion, keeper not meeting the ball). `game.js` now drives `shared/matchview.js`; squads are `[{ c, home: slotUV(slot), star }]` (`slotUV` lives in `data.js`). Each board unit gets a unique sprite id (`id#uid@team`) so duplicates are found correctly.
- **Cause before effect.** Every log line, the score, the GOAL banner, confetti and shake now fire inside the verb's `onHit` (the contact frame). Before, a possession's log was written before its beats, and goals fired on a fixed 0.45 s delay.
- **Corners.** The sim no longer bolts corner beats onto a random possession. A corner is its own possession: `build` → `view.corner` → `setShooter({ header })` → `shot`.
- **Why it happened.** `sim.js` records every synergy and link bonus (`src`) and tags each beat with the biggest one behind it (`cause()`). The pitch label and log name it ("🤝 Dima+Tomo → BLOCKED!"). When the cause is a link, its own player makes the tackle; a Playmaker feed makes the fed forward shoot. The half-time shop and the full-time sheet list goals with causes and the top synergies per side.
- **Shooter.** The player already on the ball after a counter or a run through the line takes the shot, so the ball is not passed to someone else first.
- **Tuning.** `chanceXg` raised (0.12 → 0.15 base) so fewer halves end with no shot on target.
- **Layout (375/390 px).** Removed the sticky bottom bar that covered the bench. Kick off now sits in the action row with reroll and level up, above the shop. Board, bench and unit-info rows are shorter and fixed-height, synergy chips sit on one scrolling row, and link hints are short chips. On the match screen the speed buttons moved off the pitch, a two-line ticker shows the last contact, and the pitch size is fixed once per half to fit the screen. The `.ghost` drag class no longer hijacks the Scout button.

### Review fixes
- **No blink on taps.** `draftScreen()` patches the existing screen in place (`morph`). The fade runs only on a real screen change (`draftScreen(true)`). A bought card flies to its bench slot, and a placed or swapped unit flies to its slot (`fly`).
- **Half-time strip** is one line: score, +4 gold and the synergy that did the most work, with "Why ▸" opening the full summary. The draft rows are shorter, so the shop fits at 375×812.
- **Names.** A rival unit that you also field plays under another name and hair (`oppBoard`; the sim uses `nameOf`). Every name in the ticker and log carries a kit-coloured dot. Summary goal lines name the side.
- **Truthful causes.** The sim gives each beat a ranked list (`causes`). A trait's cause names its holders. The game credits the first cause whose players touched the ball in that possession (a link needs both). The tackler is the nearest such player. The Playmaker-feed shooter rule is gone: the player on the ball shoots.
- **One label per contact.** View labels are off (`label: ''`). The game draws one combined label in `onHit` (`mark`), with the shot odds attached ("→ through! · shot 17%"). A label that follows another within its life is lifted above it.
- **Copy.** Key names in toasts only where `(hover: hover)`. Key hints are hidden in the action row under 440 px. The out-of-position toast fires for both ends of a swap. The fatigue note says it needs an Engine pair.
- **Tuning** (Node, 400 halves per case, outside the repo): `progressP` floor 0.05 → 0.28 (`0.28 + 0.62·sig((Atk−Def)/4.5)`). Halves with fewer than 2 shots: up to 34% → at most 11% (vs Catenaccio 4). The projection is now the exact score distribution from the sim's own per-possession odds (it was Poisson). The steal-shot term was fixed to match the sim (Gegenpress 4+ only). Projected win matches simulated win within about 1 point.
